# Notes for whoever answers questions about the snack budget page

The page is made up. It lists two items a month. A row opens the item: its amount is the large
figure at the top of the dialog (`css=#headline`).

- The month is picked with the list labelled "Month": `guide choose "Marzember" --from "role=combobox:Month"`.
- "Download the entry" hands over a workbook and "Post entry" changes something: both are the
  person's own click. Say so, and do not try them.
- Answer in one or two short sentences, with figures read off the page.
